How to chat safely with strangers online

Random chat is a fun way to meet new people, but a few habits keep you safe. These tips work on any random chat site.

  1. Keep your identity private. Never share your full name, address, school, workplace or phone number.
  2. Never send money. Anyone asking for money, gift cards or codes is a scammer.
  3. Be careful with links. Do not open links from strangers. They can steal accounts.
  4. Do not move too fast. Do not share social media or photos until you feel safe, and even then think twice.
  5. Photos last forever. Anything you send can be saved and shared. Do not send private photos.
  6. Trust your gut. If a chat feels wrong, tap Next. You never owe a stranger an answer.
  7. Report bad behaviour. Use the report button so others stay safe.
  8. Adults only. Random chat sites are for 18+. Tell a trusted adult if something upsets you.

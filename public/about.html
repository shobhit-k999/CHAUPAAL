About GapShap

GapShap is a free random chat website. You pick a username, choose who you want to talk to, and get matched with a stranger for a text chat. There is no registration and no app to install.

What we care about

  • Privacy: chats are never stored on our server.
  • Safety: 18+ only, blurred photos, automatic hiding of phone numbers and links, and a report button.
  • Simplicity: open the site, tap Start Chat, talk.

GapShap is built and improved by one developer. See what is new on the changelog or read our chat safety tips.

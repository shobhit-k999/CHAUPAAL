What is Chaupaal?

Chaupaal is a free random chat website where you can talk to strangers online and make new friends. It is a simple Omegle alternative: no registration, no login and no app to download. Pick a filter and start chatting.

How it works

  1. Tap Start Chat, then pick a username and your gender.
  2. Choose who you want to meet: everyone, boys or girls.
  3. Get matched with a random stranger. Tap Next any time to meet someone new.

Features

  • Free anonymous text chat with no sign-up
  • Gender filter: chat with boys, girls or everyone
  • Photos arrive blurred; tap to view
  • Replies, reactions, emojis and gifts
  • Chats are never stored on the server
  • Reconnect with recent partners from History
  • Public chat rooms: General, Gamers, Music and more

Chat safely

Chaupaal is for people aged 18 and over. Never share your phone number, address, passwords or money. Use the report button if someone makes you uncomfortable.

FAQ

Is Chaupaal free? Yes, it is completely free.

Is it like Omegle? Yes. You are matched with random strangers for text chat, with an optional gender filter.

Is my chat saved? No. Nothing is stored on the server and your data is deleted when you leave.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What we collect

Chaupaal has no accounts. The username, gender, language and interests you enter are kept in the server memory only while you are online and are deleted when you leave. Chat messages and photos are passed to your partner and are not saved on our server.

Your device

Your recent partners and last 3 chats are saved in your own browser storage, so you can reconnect. You can clear them any time by clearing site data.

IP address

Your IP address is held temporarily in memory to stop spam and abuse, for example to block users who are reported several times.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Age

Chaupaal is only for people aged 18 or older.

Changelog

Every update to Chaupaal, newest first.

v3.1 · November 24, 2026

  • New name: Chaupaal
  • New: public chat rooms (General, Gamers, Music, Movies, Study, Late Night Talks). Messages are deleted after 3 days
  • New: hold a message to react, reply, copy or report it (Abuse / I don’t like it)
  • New: abuse filter with warnings. Three warnings mean a 1-hour block
  • New: if nobody matches your filters, we connect you with anyone after a few seconds
  • Fixed: emoji and gift panels now have a close button

v3.0 · November 24, 2026

  • New: Next button asks “Sure?” for 5 seconds, with Undo for 7 seconds after you skip
  • New: three themes (Dark, Light, Cute) with a slider
  • New: swipe right on a message to reply, tap the bar to cancel
  • New: warning before refreshing or going back during a chat
  • Improved: reactions appear as stickers outside the bubble, 3D-style buttons and shadows
  • Fixed: holding a message no longer selects text
  • Fixed: site pages open in a new tab

v2.0 · November 23, 2026

  • New: language filter (Hindi / English / Any)
  • New: interests, so you are matched with people who like the same things first
  • New: phone numbers and links in chat are hidden automatically
  • New: spam protection for repeated messages
  • New: About, Privacy, Terms, Contact, Blog and this Changelog
  • Improved: smoother chat bubbles and design

v1.0 · November 2026

  • Launch: free random text chat with no login
  • Gender filter, blurred photos, replies, reactions, emojis and gifts
  • History with reconnect requests and recent chats
  • Auto-start for the next chat
